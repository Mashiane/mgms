About MGMS Holdings

Capital with a wider purpose.

MGMS Holdings is a 100% Black-owned investment company headquartered in South Africa, with a vision to transform Africa’s economic landscape through sustainable and inclusive investments.

Our focus spans six sectors that are central to Africa’s long-term resilience and growth: Agriculture, Energy, Hospitality, Logistics, Technology and Financial Services.

Our ambition extends beyond financial returns. We seek to build generational wealth, empower communities and drive innovation across the continent.
